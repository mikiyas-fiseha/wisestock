## 1. Product Overview

**Wise Shop Tracker (ብልህ Shop Tracker)** is an all-in-one cloud inventory management, Point of Sale (POS), sales tracking, and expense accounting platform designed specifically for Ethiopian retail businesses, supermarkets, pharmacies, electronics stores, boutiques, and wholesalers.

Traditional shop management in Ethiopia often suffers from:
* Missing stock and untracked inventory shrinkage.
* Lost paper receipts and unrecorded customer debts.
* No visibility into daily net profit after shop rent, wages, and operating expenses.
* Complex foreign ERP software that costs thousands of dollars and does not support local payment rails (CBE, Telebirr) or local languages.

Wise Shop Tracker solves this by providing a lightweight, modern web and mobile platform localized in **Amharic (አማርኛ)**, **English**, **Afaan Oromoo**, **Tigrinya (ትግርኛ)**, and **Somali (Af-Soomaali)** with native Ethiopian Birr (**ETB / ብር**) currency and tax support.

## 4. How to Use the Product (Step-by-Step Manual)

### Step 1: Account Registration & Company Setup
1. Visit the app URL in your browser (e.g. `https://your-domain.com/register` or local web address).
2. Enter your **Company Name**, **Your Name**, **Email**, and **Password**.
3. Click **Create Account**. Your company workspace and administrative profile are generated automatically.

### Step 2: Activating Your Subscription
1. Upon your first sign-in, you will be guided to the **Payment Plan** page.
2. The **Pro Annual Plan (2,999 ETB / Year)** will be displayed.
3. Click **Upgrade Now** to open the interactive **Payment Guide**.
4. Select your preferred payment channel:
   * **CBE (Commercial Bank of Ethiopia)**: `1000300692382`
   * **Telebirr**: `0939393770`
   * **Bank of Abyssinia**: `187437008`
5. Copy the account number with one click and make the transfer from your banking app.
6. Take a screenshot or photo of the payment confirmation and click **Attach Receipt / Proof**.
7. Submit the receipt. Your account enters review status, and the Super Admin will approve your annual access immediately.

### Step 3: Setting Up Branches & Team Permissions
1. Navigate to **Settings** $\rightarrow$ **Branches**.
2. Add your physical shop locations (e.g., *"Bole Branch - Main Store"*, *"Merkato Branch - Wholesale"*).
3. Navigate to **Settings** $\rightarrow$ **Team Members**:
   * Add accounts for your cashiers and store managers.
   * Assign appropriate roles: **Cashier/Sales** (sales only) or **Manager** (inventory + sales).

### Step 4: Building Your Inventory & Product Catalog
1. Go to the **Products** tab and click **Add Product** (or **+**).
2. Enter the product details:
   * **Name**: (e.g., *"Samsung Galaxy A15 128GB"* or *"Men's Classic Shirt"*).
   * **Barcode / SKU**: Scan with your handheld barcode gun or enter manually.
   * **Cost Price (ETB)**: How much you bought it for (used to calculate net profit).
   * **Selling Price (ETB)**: Retail price for customers.
   * **Stock Quantity**: Current count on your shelves.
   * **Category**: Group items (e.g., Electronics, Food, Apparel).
   * **Minimum Alert Threshold**: (e.g., `5` units)—the app will alert you when stock falls below this level.
3. Save the product.

### Step 5: Processing Sales via POS (Point of Sale)
1. Open the **Sales** tab and click **New Sale** (or the cashier register icon).
2. **Add Products to Cart**:
   * Scan an item's barcode with your barcode gun (it will be added immediately).
   * OR type the product name/SKU in the search bar and click it.
3. Adjust quantities, add item discounts if applicable.
4. Select the **Customer**:
   * Leave as **Walk-in Customer** for everyday cash shoppers.
   * OR select/create a registered customer for credit sales.
5. Choose the **Payment Method**:
   * `Cash`, `Telebirr`, `CBE Birr`, `Bank Transfer`, or `Credit / Debt`.
6. Enter **Amount Received** $\rightarrow$ the app calculates change automatically.
7. Click **Complete Sale**:
   * The receipt dialog opens automatically.
   * Click **Print** to send the receipt to your 80mm/58mm thermal printer, or click **Share** to send a digital PDF.

### Step 6: Tracking Operating Expenses
1. Go to the **Expenses** tab and click **Add Expense**.
2. Select the category (e.g., *Rent*, *Utilities*, *Salaries*, *Transport*, *Supplies*).
3. Enter the amount paid in ETB.
4. Optionally snap or attach a photo of the paper receipt/bill.
5. Click **Save Expense** $\rightarrow$ your profit calculations update automatically.

### Step 7: Managing Customer Debts & Credit Sales
1. To view outstanding debts, go to **Reports** $\rightarrow$ **Debts** (or the **Customers** tab).
2. The list displays customers with negative balances and payment due dates.
3. When a customer brings cash or sends Telebirr to settle their debt:
   * Click on the customer profile.
   * Click **Collect Payment**.
   * Enter the amount paid and select the payment channel.
   * Confirm the payment $\rightarrow$ their debt balance decreases and an official repayment receipt is generated.

### Step 8: Managing Suppliers & Purchases
1. When new stock arrives from your distributor:
2. Open the **Purchases** tab and click **New Purchase**.
3. Select the supplier, pick the products delivered, and enter the purchase quantities and cost.
4. If payment was deferred, mark as **Unpaid/Credit** to add to your Accounts Payable.
5. Save the purchase $\rightarrow$ your warehouse stock levels increase automatically.

### Step 9: Analytics, Reports & Financial Audits
1. Open the **Dashboard** or **Reports** tab to monitor business health:
   * **Gross Sales & Net Profit**: Gross revenue minus cost of goods sold and operating expenses.
   * **Top Selling Products**: Identifies your best-performing inventory.
   * **Inventory Valuation**: Total monetary value of stock currently on your shelves.
2. Click **Export PDF** or **Export Excel** to download executive audit files for accountants or tax filing.

## 6. Hardware & Device Setup

### A. Cashier PCs, Laptops & Tablets (Desktop Web)
* **Browser**: Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge.
* **PWA Installation**: In the browser address bar, click the **Install** button (or Menu $\rightarrow$ *Install Wise Shop Tracker*).
* **Shortcut**: The app will sit on the desktop taskbar and launch in a dedicated, full-screen POS window.

### B. Barcode Scanner Setup
* Use any standard USB or wireless 2.4GHz handheld barcode scanner.
* **Configuration**: Ensure the scanner is set to its factory default "Keyboard Wedge" mode with an automatic **Enter/Return** suffix.
* When you scan an item in the POS search bar, it immediately types the barcode and presses Enter, adding the product to the cart in less than 100 milliseconds.

### C. Thermal Receipt Printer Setup
* **Compatible Sizes**: Standard 58mm (2-inch) and 80mm (3-inch) USB, Bluetooth, or LAN ESC/POS receipt printers (e.g., Epson, Xprinter, Rongta).
* In Windows/Mac, set the thermal printer as your default printer, with margins set to **None**.

## 7. Technical Specifications

* **App Name**: Wise Shop Tracker (ብልህ Shop Tracker)
* **Frontend**: React Native with Expo SDK 54 & Expo Router v6
* **Web Engine**: Static Progressive Web App (PWA) with Service Worker caching
* **Backend Database**: PostgreSQL 15 via Supabase with Row Level Security (RLS)
* **Hosting Targets**: Vercel, Netlify, Cloudflare Pages, or on-premise Nginx
* **Encryption & Security**: HTTPS/TLS 1.3, salted bcrypt authentication, and isolated tenant databases.
