# 🎬 Wise Stock — Video Ad Script
### Concept: "The Cash Flow Reality Check"
* **Target Audience**: Ethiopian shop owners, boutique owners, electronics/spare-part retailers, wholesalers, pharmacy & supermarket managers.
* **Format**: 9:16 Vertical Video (TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, Telegram).
* **Duration**: 45 – 60 seconds.
* **Character**: A confident, approachable young businesswoman / shop manager sitting at a desk or modern shop counter, speaking directly to the camera.

---

## 📌 Scene Breakdown & Production Guide

### [0:00 – 0:08] The Hook (Scroll-Stopping Reality Check)
* **Visual**: Medium close-up. The presenter sits calmly at a counter with a notebook and calculator pushed aside. She leans slightly forward, looking straight into the lens with serious, empathetic eyes.
* **Audio / Music**: Soft, intriguing low synth pulse (stops the scroll).
* **On-Screen Text (Captions)**: *"ለምንድነው አብዛኛው ሱቅ የሚከስረው? 🛑"*

**Voiceover / Dialogue (Amharic)**:
> "ታውቃላችሁ? አብዛኛው የንግድ ድርጅት የሚከስረው ደንበኛ ወይም ገበያ ጠፍቶ አይደለም... **Poor Cash Flow Management** — ወይም የገንዘብና የዕቃ ፍሰቱን በአግባቡ ባለመቆጣጠሩ ነው!"

**English Translation**:
> "Did you know? Most businesses don't fail because they lack customers... they fail because of poor cash flow management — not knowing where their money and inventory are actually going!"

**Voiceover / Dialogue (Amharic)**:
> "ቀኑን ሙሉ ይደክማሉ... በየቀኑ ሽያጭ አለ... ገንዘብ ይገባል፣ ይወጣል።

> * የትኛው ዕቃ በብዛት እንዳለቀ፣ የትኛው ደግሞ ተኝቶ እንደቀረ አይለዩም!

## 💡 Director & Creator Tips for Best Performance

1. **Energy & Delivery**:
   - The first 15 seconds should feel like an honest friend giving urgent financial advice—not an aggressive sales pitch.
   - Pacing should be confident, natural, and relatable.

2. **Styling & Setting**:
   - Setting: A clean shop desk (boutique, cosmetics, electronics, or retail counter).
   - Props: A physical notebook + calculator at the beginning $\rightarrow$ replaced by a phone or laptop with Wise Stock open.

3. **Subtitles / Captions**:
   - 80% of viewers watch social media videos on mute! Use large, colorful kinetic subtitles (CapCut or Premiere) highlighting keywords like **Cash Flow**, **ትርፍ (Profit)**, **Wise Stock**.
